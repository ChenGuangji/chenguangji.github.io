Research programme

From genomes to avian diversity

My work connects phylogenomics, comparative genomics, genome evolution and complex phenotypic evolution to understand the origins of bird diversity.

01

Radiation & speciation

Reconstructing the difficult branches of the avian tree of life and identifying processes that produced rapid diversification.

02

Genome evolution & complex phenotypic evolution

Studying endogenous retroviruses, transposable elements, structural genomic change and the evolution of complex phenotypes across bird lineages.
